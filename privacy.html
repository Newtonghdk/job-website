Privacy

This website loads job listings and sponsored content from a Google Apps Script service. Requests are processed by Google and the website’s hosting provider.

Saved jobs

Saving a job stores its identifier in your browser’s local storage. No account is required. You can remove saved jobs individually or clear this website’s browser data. Saved jobs do not sync across devices.

Applications and external content

Applications open employer websites. Those websites have their own privacy policies. Employer logos and advertising images may load from external servers, which receive normal browser request information.

Information collected by this site

The website does not include registration forms, application uploads, analytics scripts or advertising trackers. Your searches are filtered in your browser and are not submitted to a search service.

For enquiries about this website, use the contact channels published on NEIC’s website.
